# Constraints — generic-fantasy

<!-- Hand-written, ~1 page. This catches ~80% of what breaks immersion. Iterate freely;

## Voice register

Third person close, dry, understated. Character through behaviour, never through the
narrator's opinions. Violence is factual, not lingered on. Humour is deadpan and belongs
to the characters, not the narrator.

## Naming per faction

- Villagers: plain, worn names, one or two syllables (Marrowfield pattern: place names are
  compound and slightly ominous).
- Mercenaries and adventurers: short personal names, no surnames unless earned (Vesh, Corin).
- Goblins and the like: never named individually unless one matters; then a name that sounds
  overheard, not invented.
- Nothing that sounds like a modern name. Nothing with apostrophes.

## Hard rules

- No resurrection. The dead stay dead.
- Magic is rare, costly, and never fully understood by anyone present.
- No prophecy, no chosen ones. Things happen because people decide things.
- The world does not care about the party. It was here first.

## Forbidden anachronisms

"okay", "guys", "adrenaline", "seconds/minutes" as time units, percentages, "training" as
an abstraction, modern psychology vocabulary ("trauma", "stress"), any metric units.
Distances in paces, days, or the reach of a bow. Time by light, meals, and watches.

## Scale calibration

A village is thirty houses. A big fight is a dozen on each side. A famous sword is famous
in one valley. A long journey is a week on foot. Keep numbers small enough to picture.
