# Tone — kids

Same craft as the register sample. Same events. Different consequence handling.

- **Consequences:** everything still happens — falls, fights, losses — but the camera cuts
  on impact rather than dwelling. Aftermath shows in behaviour (a limp, a quiet evening),
  not in description of harm. Death of named characters does not happen on stage; if the
  spec's given beats include one, it is discovered, not witnessed.
- **Stakes framing:** danger is real and felt through the POV character's decisions, not
  through dread. Fear is allowed; hopelessness is not.
- **Humour:** more room for it, still deadpan, still belonging to the characters.
- **Ending posture:** earned warmth. Not a moral, not a lesson — the characters are all
  right, and something small is better than it was.
- **Language:** simpler sentence rhythm, no simpler ideas. Never condescend; the reader
  is clever and eight.
