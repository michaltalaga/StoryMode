Session notes, Marrowfield. GM-side memory, quick and dirty.

Opened with the elder. Played him stingy — wouldn't let the party in the house, door half
closed the whole conversation. Gem stolen, it's in the caves above the treeline, village
"will be grateful". Vesh's player immediately suspicious about payment, good scene, let it
breathe.

Caves. Three traps prepared, they found the first two easily (pressure plate, falling
stone). Third one — false wall. Corin leaned on it with the lamp and went straight through,
fell into a lower grotto. Rolled damage, kept him alive, but he was separated for a good
chunk. I improvised the grotto since they went off my map — didn't have anything prepared
for how he gets back up, we sort of handwaved it to keep things moving. Worth inventing
something better for the story.

Then the goblin fight at the underground lake. Longest part of the evening by far, they
loved it. Corin did the distraction on the chief (the burning lamp oil thing), Vesh flanked.
Clean win, nobody down.

Gem recovered, back to the village, elder says thank you and precisely nothing else. Vesh's
player: "grateful". Table lost it.

Skip: twenty minutes of arguing about initiative order during the goblin fight, and the
sandwich break in the middle of the cave section.
